# Initial Concept
Project Weather is a comprehensive agricultural utility platform designed to help farmers and agricultural professionals make informed decisions based on meteorological data and crop-specific logic.

# Product Guide: Project Weather

## Product Vision
To empower farmers and agricultural professionals with precision weather intelligence, automated crop health & growth insights, risk warnings, and actionable field management tools to maximize crop yield and minimize weather-related risks.

## Target Audience
- **Farmers & Field Managers:** Require mobile-first, easy-to-understand real-time weather analytics, field plot tracking, irrigation recommendations, disease risk alerts, and growth stage estimates.
- **Agricultural Administrators & Extension Workers:** Require system-wide dashboards to monitor regional crop health, send broadcast typhoon/weather alerts, manage digital repositories, and oversee user activity.

## Key Features & Capabilities
1. **Real-Time Weather Monitoring & Analytics:**
   - Live temperature, humidity, wind speed, precipitation metrics.
   - Interactive charts and forecasts powered by Google Weather API and local weather log data.
2. **Crop & Field Plot Tracking:**
   - Manage multi-plot farm layouts, soil profiles, planting records, and growing degree day (GDD) calculations.
3. **Automated Agricultural Decision Support:**
   - Rule-based decision models for disease risk estimation, fertilizer timing, smart irrigation schedules, and multi-factor Safe-to-Plant prediction engine.
4. **Early Warning & Alert System:**
   - Automated notifications for unseasonal heat, extreme rainfall, drought risks, and typhoon alerts via dashboard and OTP/email notifications.
5. **Digital Knowledge Repository & Trivia:**
   - Centralized agricultural knowledge base, crop varieties guide, and interactive trivia for learning best practices.
6. **Multi-Role Authentication & Administration:**
   - Role-based control (Farmer vs. Admin) with secure session handling, OTP verification, and profile management.
