# Product Guidelines: Project Weather

## Design & UI Principles
- **Mobile First & Field-Ready:** Interfaces must be responsive, high-contrast, and clear under daylight outdoor conditions.
- **Data Density & Visualization:** Present weather charts, humidity meters, and crop risk levels clearly using intuitive color coding (Green = Normal, Yellow = Caution, Red = High Risk/Alert).
- **Aesthetic Excellence:** Use vibrant, cohesive HSL color schemes, modern typography, glassmorphic cards, and smooth micro-animations for high perceived quality.

## UX & Accessibility Guidelines
- **Zero-Friction Navigation:** Core field actions (viewing weather, checking plot status, logging crop data, checking alerts) must be reachable within 1-2 taps.
- **Status Visibility:** Always surface current connectivity, sync state, and pending weather alerts at the top of main views.
- **Form Design:** Pre-fill reasonable defaults for location, soil type, and crop parameters to minimize manual data entry for field users.

## Tone & Communication Style
- **Clear & Action-Oriented:** Notifications and recommendations should use direct, actionable phrasing (e.g., "High Risk of Fungal Infection — Apply Fungicide within 24h").
- **Empathetic & Professional:** Weather alerts should inform and advise without inciting unnecessary panic.
- **Jargon Mitigation:** Pair technical agricultural terms (e.g., Growing Degree Days, Evapotranspiration) with plain-language explanations or tooltip definitions.
